# ADR-0001: End-Load Allocation by Room Water Temperature Tier

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-09
**Decision Makers:** Architect, Domain Expert, PM

---

## Context

In the original v0.5 design, all terminal loads were simplified to be allocated to the "mid-temperature" (12/17°C) chilled water tier. This was a simplification to reduce complexity.

However, in real PCB factories, different rooms have different cooling requirements:
- Some rooms require low-temperature chilled water (7/12°C) for precise temperature control
- Other rooms can use mid-temperature chilled water (12/17°C) for general cooling
- The allocation affects equipment sizing and system design

## Decision

**Override the "all to mid-temp" simplification.**

Terminal loads will be allocated at the **room level** to a specific chilled water tier:
- Each room has a `water_tier_id` field that determines which chilled water tier its terminal load belongs to
- Default allocation: Mid-temperature (12/17°C)
- Users can override per room to low-temperature (7/12°C) or custom tiers

## Rationale

1. **Accuracy:** Different rooms genuinely need different water temperatures
2. **Equipment Sizing:** Correct allocation ensures proper chiller sizing
3. **Flexibility:** Allows users to model real-world scenarios accurately
4. **Backward Compatible:** Default behavior matches the simplified approach

## Consequences

### Positive
- More accurate load calculations
- Better equipment sizing recommendations
- Supports real-world PCB factory configurations

### Negative
- Slightly increased complexity in UI (dropdown per room)
- Users must understand water tier concepts

### Risks
- Users may incorrectly allocate tiers → Mitigated by clear defaults and validation

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Keep "All to Mid-Temp" Simplification
**Rejected:** Inaccurate for factories with mixed cooling requirements

### Alternative 2: Auto-Allocation Based on Room Type
**Rejected:** Too complex for v0.5, room types not yet modeled
