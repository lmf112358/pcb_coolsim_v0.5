## Project Context

### Project Overview

**PCB-CoolSim** is a web-based platform for calculating cooling loads in PCB (Printed Circuit Board) manufacturing factories. It replaces manual Excel-based calculations with an automated, visual, and collaborative tool.

### Target Users

| Role | Description | Primary Tasks |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| **暖通工程师 (HVAC Engineer)** | Core user, designs cooling systems | Data entry, calculation, analysis |
| **项目经理 (Project Manager)** | Manages multiple projects | Review, export, reporting |
| **系统管理员 (System Admin)** | Manages system and users | Configuration, user management |
| **查看者 (Viewer)** | Reviews designs | View-only access |

### Business Domain

PCB manufacturing involves multiple processes that generate significant heat:
- **Etching** (chemical processes)
- **Plating** (electrochemical processes)
- **Lamination** (high temperature pressing)
- **Drilling** (mechanical heat)
- **Clean rooms** (strict environmental control)

Each process requires specific cooling, making accurate load calculation critical for:
- Equipment sizing (chillers, AHUs, FCUs)
- Energy efficiency
- Process quality control

## Glossary

### A

**ACH (Air Changes per Hour)**
换气次数 - Number of times air is replaced in a room per hour. Used for ventilation calculations.

**AHU (Air Handling Unit)**
空调箱 - Central air conditioning unit that conditions and distributes air.

**Area (面积)**
Room floor area in square meters (m²). Used for load calculations.

### C

**Chiller (冷冻机/冷机)**  ⚠️ *v0.5 不实现冷站冷机选型（模块五冷站已移除），仅作通用 HVAC 术语保留*
Machine that produces chilled water for cooling. Types:
- Centrifugal (离心式)
- Screw (螺杆式)
- Scroll (涡旋式)

**Chilled Water Tier (冷冻水档)**
Classification by water temperature:
- Low Temperature (低温): 7/12°C supply/return
- Mid Temperature (中温): 12/17°C supply/return

**City Config (城市配置)**
National standard outdoor design parameters for each city, including temperature, humidity, atmospheric pressure.

**Clean Room (洁净室)**
Controlled environment room with specific cleanliness levels (ISO 1-9).

**Coefficient Table (比例系数表)**
Matrix of multipliers used in dynamic simulation to convert static loads to hourly loads.

**Cooling Load (冷负荷)**
Amount of heat that must be removed to maintain desired conditions. Measured in kW or RT.

**对话式采集 (Conversational Acquisition)**
以自然语言对话为主、结构化侧栏为辅的数据采集通道，与树形/Excel 录入同源（见 PRD §6.10）。

**CW (Cooling Water)**
冷却水 - Water used to reject heat from chillers to cooling towers.

### D

**Design Load (设计负荷)**
Maximum cooling load under worst-case conditions (summer design day).

**Dew Point (露点)**
Temperature at which moisture condenses from air.

**Dry Bulb Temperature (干球温度)**
Air temperature measured by standard thermometer.

### E

**Enthalpy (焓)**
Total heat content of moist air. Measured in kJ/kg. Used for fresh air load calculation.

**Extra Load (额外负荷)**
Cooling loads not associated with specific rooms:
- PCW (Process Cooling Water)
- Electrical room cooling
- Other special loads

**Exhaust Air (排风)**
Air removed from rooms. Types:
- Heat exhaust (热排风)
- Acid exhaust (酸性排风)
- Alkaline exhaust (碱性排风)
- Organic exhaust (有机排风)
- Dust exhaust (含尘排风)

### F

**FCU (Fan Coil Unit)**
风机盘管 - Room-level air conditioning unit.

**Floor (楼层)**
Building level. One building can have multiple floors.

**Fresh Air (新风)**
Outside air brought into the building for ventilation.

**Fresh Air Load (新风冷负荷)**
Cooling load from conditioning outside air to indoor conditions.

### H

**HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning)**
暖通空调 - Building mechanical systems for environmental control.

### I

**Infiltration Air (渗透风)**
Unintentional air leakage through building envelope due to pressure differences.

### L

**Latent Heat (潜热)**
Heat associated with moisture content in air.

**Load Density (负荷密度)**
Cooling load per unit area, measured in W/m².

### M

**Material (材质)**
Building material properties affecting heat transfer.

### N

**National Standard (国标)**
Chinese national standards for building design:
- GB 50019: Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning Design
- GB 50736: Civil Building Ventilation and Air Conditioning Design

### P

**PCW (Process Cooling Water)**
工艺冷却水 - Chilled water used directly in manufacturing processes.

**PLR (Part Load Ratio)**  ⚠️ *v0.5 不实现（绑定冷机容量的部分负荷率，随模块五冷站移除而搁置，v0.6 评估）*
部分负荷率 - Ratio of actual load to chiller capacity.

### R

**Relative Humidity (相对湿度)**
Percentage of moisture in air relative to saturation.

**Room (功能区域)**
Smallest calculation unit in the system. Represents a functional area in the factory.

**RT (Refrigeration Ton)**
冷吨 - Unit of cooling capacity. 1 RT = 3.517 kW.

### S

**Sensible Heat (显热)**
Heat that causes temperature change without moisture change.

**Simulation (仿真)**
Dynamic calculation of hourly loads over extended periods (typically 8760 hours × multiple years).

**Static Calculation (静态计算)**
Design load calculation using worst-case conditions (summer design day).

### T

**Terminal Load (末端负荷)**
Cooling load handled by room-level equipment (AHU, FCU). Includes:
- Civil load (土建负荷): From building envelope
- Lighting load (照明负荷): From lighting
- Personnel load (人员负荷): From people
- Equipment load (设备负荷): From machinery

### V

**Volume (体积)**
Room volume in cubic meters (m³). Calculated as: area × height.

### W

**Wet Bulb Temperature (湿球温度)**
Temperature measured by thermometer with wetted bulb. Indicates moisture content.

**Water Temperature Config (冷冻水温度配置)**
Project-level configuration of chilled water supply and return temperatures.

### 8

**8760 Hours (8760小时)**
Total hours in a non-leap year. Used for annual energy analysis.

### v0.5.1 补充术语（架构修订新增）

**末端冷冻水档 (Terminal Chilled-Water Tier)**
功能区域末端负荷归属的冷冻水温度档（ADR-0001）。默认中温 12/17°C，用户可逐区指定为低温 7/12°C。存储于 `Room.terminal_water_temp_config_id`。

**温控精度 (Temperature Control Precision)**
功能区域温度控制精度等级，枚举值 ±1 / ±2 / ±3 / ±5 / 无要求。参与工艺色编码判定（PRD F3-016，最高优先级）。存储于 `Room.temp_precision`。

**工艺色编码 (Process Color Coding)**
2D 平面图双模式颜色编码之一（PRD F3-015/F3-016）。按优先级判定：温控精度 > 洁净度 > 压差，7 档色：①±1°C 红 ②±2°C 橙 ③ISO7 黄 ④ISO8 绿 ⑤ISO7.5 蓝 ⑥正压 紫 ⑦负压/普通 灰。SAC-6 验收依据。

**压差渗透风量 (Pressure Differential Infiltration)**
因功能区域内外压差通过缝隙渗透的空气量。v0.5.1 使用 8 点查表线性插值（PRD §8.3 唯一权威表）：0Pa→0, |±1|→1.0, |±2|→1.5, |±3|→2.0, |±5|→3.0, |±10|→3.5, |±15|→4.0, >|±15| 封顶 4.0；正负压对称取绝对值。公式：`infiltration = room.volume × k(|pressure_diff|)`。

**新风冷负荷 (Fresh Air Cooling Load)**
将室外新风处理到室内状态所需的冷量。公式：`Q_fresh = ρ × V_fresh × (h_outdoor − h_indoor) / 3600`，ρ=DefaultConfig.air_density 默认 1.2 kg/m³，焓值用 psychrolib。负荷可为负（冬季室外焓低于室内）；总负荷 < 0 截断为 0。

**比例系数法 (Ratio Coefficient Method)**
动态仿真方式二与负荷预测共用算法（ADR-0003）。查 `RatioCoefficient` 统一权威表，动态仿真取负荷率=100% 列按室外温度查表，负荷预测用全表（温度×负荷率二维双线性插值）。

**对话式采集 (Conversational Data Collection)**
PRD §6.10 新增的第三种数据录入通道（与 Tab1 树形、Excel 导入并列）。系统按 SOP 六阶段主动提问，用户自然语言作答，回写同一 Room 单表。会话状态落 `ConversationSession`/`ConversationMessage`（ADR-0007），支持断点续采（F2-057）。

**断点续采 (Resume from Checkpoint)**
对话式采集的特性：会话状态服务端持久化，刷新或重开浏览器后从上次未完成节点继续（F2-057）。

**26280**
3 年 × 8760 小时 ≈ 26280（含闰年约 26304），代表动态仿真三年逐时时间序列。每功能区域约 26280 个逐时负荷点。

**LTTB (Largest-Triangle-Three-Buckets)**
时间序列降采样算法。前端 26280 点图表渲染时用 LTTB 降采样到 2000~5000 点，保证 < 3s 渲染（NF-SAC-07）且保留波形特征。

**虚拟滚动 (Virtual Scrolling)**
前端长列表性能优化技术。树形导航 100,000+ 功能区域节点（全项目合计，NF-001）使用虚拟滚动，仅渲染可视区域节点，< 2s 加载交互。

**装机容量校核 (Installed Capacity Validation)**
模块七基础验证（PRD F7-022~024）。负荷持续时间曲线 + 装机容量线，校验：①充分性（全年峰值 ≤ 装机容量）；②经济性（避免全年 > 90% 时间负荷率 < 50%）。不通过红色警告 + 修改建议。

**四级继承 (Four-Level Inheritance)**
默认值配置的覆盖优先级（PRD F2-041/F9-009）：功能区域显式值 > 楼层级 > 建筑级 > 项目级 > 系统默认值。

**TimescaleDB 超表 (Hypertable)**
PostgreSQL 扩展 TimescaleDB 的分区表机制。`DynamicLoadHourly`（动态仿真逐时结果）与 `WeatherRecord`（逐时气象）建为超表，按时间 range 分区，支撑 26280×1000 量级数据 < 30s 查询（NF-006）。

## Domain-Specific Formulas

### Enthalpy Calculation
```
h = 1.006 × T + W × (2501 + 1.86 × T)

Where:
h = enthalpy (kJ/kg)
T = dry bulb temperature (°C)
W = humidity ratio (kg/kg)
```

### Terminal Load
```
Terminal Load = Civil Load + Lighting Load + Personnel Load + Equipment Load

Where:
Civil Load = Area × Civil Index
Lighting Load = Area × Lighting Index
Personnel Load = Personnel Count × Personnel Load Index
Equipment Load = Σ(Power × Factor)
```

### Fresh Air Load
```
Fresh Air Load = Fresh Air Volume × Air Density × Enthalpy Difference

Where:
Fresh Air Volume = Exhaust Volume + Infiltration Volume
Air Density ≈ 1.2 kg/m³ (at standard conditions)
Enthalpy Difference = Outdoor Enthalpy - Indoor Enthalpy
```
