## Key Design Decisions

### ADR-0001: End-load Allocation
**Decision:** Allocate terminal loads at room level to specific water temperature tiers (not "all to mid-temp").

### ADR-0002: Async Tasks
**Decision:** Use Celery + Redis for long-running tasks (simulation, export, weather fetch).

### ADR-0003: Coefficient Table
**Decision:** Consolidate two coefficient tables into single authoritative matrix.

### ADR-0004: Validation V3
**Decision:** Validate operating capacity (excluding one standby) against design load.

### ADR-0005: P&ID + Water Storage
**Decision:** Include basic P&ID generation; water storage capacity only (no ROI in v0.5).
